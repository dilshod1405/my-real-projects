## Overview
A one-page marketing site that Master Homes ran for an off-plan launch on Ramhan Island (standalone beachfront villas
with a down-payment / payment-plan offer). It belongs to the same family of campaign pages as
[dubai-island](../dubai-island/) and [fairmont-downtown](../fairmont-downtown/); inquiries are delivered to the sales
team by e-mail via [smtp-service](../smtp-service/).

## Key Features
- Hero "book your home" block with offer details and register-interest call to action
- Living / unit-type cards, lifestyle section and amenities
- Master plan and location sections
- Photo carousel and background video with image placeholder while loading
- Lead form in a modal, plus a direct booking-modal route for ad links
- Responsive layout with Sass modules; static build served by Nginx in Docker
